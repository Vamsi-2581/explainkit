# A real story told in silhouettes, with the protagonist in every shot. Facts: the Imperial Trans-Antarctic
# Expedition, 1914-1916 (Endurance trapped Jan 1915, crushed Nov 1915; the James Caird voyage, April-May 1916;
# rescue of the men on Elephant Island on August 30, 1916; all 28 men of the Endurance survived).

Twenty-eight men were stranded at the bottom of the world. Every single one of them came home.

In 1914, Ernest Shackleton sailed for Antarctica. His ship, the Endurance, got trapped in the ice.
Months later, the ice crushed it.

Twenty-eight men. Three lifeboats. Nobody knew where they were.
They dragged the boats across the ice, then rowed to a barren island.

So Shackleton took five men and one small boat, and sailed about eight hundred miles through some of the roughest ocean on Earth. It took sixteen days.
Then three of them crossed the mountains of South Georgia on foot.

On August thirtieth, nineteen sixteen, he sailed back for the others.
Every one of the twenty-eight survived.

He had planned to cross a continent. When that failed, he changed the goal: bring everyone home.
