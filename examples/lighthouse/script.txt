# "DAY N" series with rules and a clock: an original second-person story in the daily-series format.
# The ending asks the audience for one concrete action (vote with one letter).

Day three. You are the new keeper of a lighthouse, on a rock in the middle of the sea.

The old keeper left you three rules.
Rule one. The lamp must never go out before the clock says six.
Rule two. If someone knocks, do not open the door.
Rule three. Never count the knocks.

Tonight, the lamp flickers.
And someone knocks. Once. Twice.
You start counting. Three. Four.

Then a voice says your name, from the other side of the door.
It sounds like your sister.

The clock says four fifty-two.
Do you open the door, or keep the lamp lit?
Comment A, open the door. Or B, keep the light on.
